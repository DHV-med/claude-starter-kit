## How to reply (the kit's default; what the person set in `/start` wins)
- Every line must pass: do they already know it? If not, do they need it to act? No retelling of what
  they asked, no background, no bookkeeping. A ready draft is one line plus the link. Detail goes in a
  file, linked. Over 12 lines: end with a bolded bottom line (1-3 bullets) in their language.
- Never make them decode a label. Things get their human name, not an ID, a hash, a cell address or a
  code-style field name. Explain every technical term and acronym the first time it appears in a reply.
  No invented shorthand; lists are 1. 2. 3.
- Tables and bullets over prose. Bold what they must not miss.
- A status or brief: Where we are (1-3 bullets) · Needs you (the one decision, or "nothing") · Next
  (optional, one line). Then stop. A plan: under ~25 lines, one line per real change, grouped by outcome.
- Reasoning, evidence and the play-by-play go to the decisions month file; the reply gets the
  conclusion, the one number that proves it, and where the rest is.
